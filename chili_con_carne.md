# Chili con Carne

- Servings: 8–10  
- Preparation: 25 minutes  
- Cooking: 75–90 minutes  
- Total Time: Approximately 2 hours

## Ingredients

    3 pounds beef steak (chuck or similar cut suitable for braising)
    1 pound ground beef (80/20 recommended)
    Kosher salt, to taste
    Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
    2 tablespoons vegetable oil
    1 large onion, finely diced
    4 medium garlic cloves, grated
    1 tablespoon ground cumin
    1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
    1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
    2 teaspoons dried oregano
    1 cup Chile Paste
    1 quart chicken stock
    2–4 tablespoons cornmeal, as needed for thickening

## Tools

- Instant Pot (8-quart recommended)
- Sharp knife and cutting board
- Microplane grater
- Large mixing bowl
- Wooden spoon
- Whisk

## Steps

1. Prepare the ingredients
   - Finely dice the onion
   - Grate the garlic using a Microplane
   - Trim excess fat/gristle from steak and cut into 2-inch chunks
   - Season the steak with salt and pepper

2. Brown the steak
   - Set the Instant Pot to Sauté (High) and add the vegetable oil
   - Brown approximately half the steak in batches, avoiding overcrowding
   - Cook without moving until well browned on the bottom, approximately 4–5 minutes
   - Transfer the browned steak to a large bowl with the remaining uncooked steak

3. Brown the ground beef
   - Add the ground beef to the Instant Pot
   - Cook for 5–7 minutes, breaking it into small pieces with a wooden spoon
   - Continue until the beef is browned and most of its moisture has evaporated
   - Transfer to the bowl with the steak
   - Drain excess fat if necessary, leaving approximately 1–2 tablespoons in the pot

4. Cook aromatics
   - Reduce the Instant Pot to Sauté (Normal)
   - Add the diced onion and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, approximately 5–6 minutes
   - Add the garlic, cumin, cinnamon, allspice, and oregano
   - Cook, stirring continuously, until fragrant, approximately 1 minute

5. Combine ingredients
   - Add approximately 1/2 cup chicken stock and scrape the bottom of the pot thoroughly to release any browned bits
   - Return the steak, ground beef, and accumulated juices to the pot
   - Add the chile paste and remaining chicken stock
   - Stir thoroughly to combine

6. Pressure cook.
   - Secure the Instant Pot lid and set the valve to Sealing
   - Select Pressure Cook (High) for 40 minutes
   - Allow 15–20 minutes of natural pressure release, then carefully release any remaining pressure
   - Check the steak for tenderness. It should separate easily with a fork
   - If necessary, pressure cook for an additional 5–10 minutes

7. Thicken chili
   - Remove the lid and select Sauté (Normal)
   - Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper
   - Mix 2 tablespoons of cornmeal with a small amount of hot cooking liquid in a separate bowl
   - Stir the mixture into the chili
   - Simmer uncovered for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally
   - Add additional cornmeal if needed to reach the desired consistency

## Notes

- Steak selection: Chuck steak is ideal because its connective tissue breaks down during pressure cooking. Other well-marbled braising cuts also work. Lean grilling steaks, such as sirloin or strip, may become dry during prolonged pressure cooking.
- Ground beef: An 80/20 blend adds richness and body. Browning it separately develops flavor and improves texture.
- Browning: Browning only half the steak provides substantial flavor while reducing preparation time. Browning all the ground beef prevents it from forming large clumps during pressure cooking.
- Stock quantity: One quart is generally sufficient for pressure cooking. Unlike conventional cooking, the Instant Pot retains nearly all its liquid. Additional stock can be added after cooking if the chili is too thick.
- Thickening: Start with 2 tablespoons of cornmeal and increase gradually. The chili will thicken further as it cools.
- Overnight improvement: Refrigerating overnight allows the flavors to develop and makes excess fat easier to remove.
- Chile paste: The flavor and heat depend heavily on the chile paste used. Adjust according to its strength.
- Storage: Refrigerate for up to 3–4 days or freeze in airtight containers for approximately 3 months
