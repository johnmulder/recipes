# Dutch Oven Pot Roast

## Ingredients

    3–4 lb chuck roast  
    2 tsp kosher salt  
    1 tsp black pepper  
    2 tbsp olive oil  
    1 large onion, sliced  
    3 cloves garlic, smashed  
    2 tbsp tomato paste  
    2 cups beef stock  
    1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce  
    2 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)  
    1 bay leaf  
    4 carrots, cut into large chunks  
    1 lb baby potatoes (or large potatoes, quartered)

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid
- Tongs
- Wooden spoon
- Knife and cutting board
- Oven mitts

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Pat the roast dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Sear the roast on all sides until deeply browned, 3–4 minutes per side.
5. Remove roast to a plate.
6. Lower heat to medium. Add onion and cook until lightly caramelized.
7. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
8. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1–2 minutes until darkened.
9. Add beef stock, Worcestershire, thyme, and bay leaf, scraping up browned bits.
10. Return roast to the pot.
11. Cover and transfer to oven.
12. Cook for 2.5–3 hours, until fork-tender.
13. Add carrots and potatoes, cover, and cook another 30–45 minutes.
14. Remove roast and vegetables; skim excess fat if desired.
15. Rest meat 10 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Chuck roast is ideal; lean cuts will dry out.
- Vegetables added too early will become mushy—timing matters.
- Sauce can be reduced on the stovetop if thicker gravy is desired.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches long, low collagen breakdown and ingredient staging.
You learn how connective tissue transforms into gelatin over time, creating
richness without added fat. Adding vegetables later prevents overcooking and
preserves texture. This timing discipline is critical for all slow braises
and stews.
