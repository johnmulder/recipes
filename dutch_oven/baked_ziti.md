# Dutch Oven Baked Ziti

## Ingredients

    1 lb ziti or rigatoni  
    1 tbsp olive oil  
    1 lb Italian sausage or ground beef  
    1 tsp kosher salt  
    ½ tsp black pepper  
    1 medium onion, diced  
    3 cloves garlic, minced  
    1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
    2 tbsp tomato paste  
    1 tsp dried oregano  
    1 tsp dried basil  
    1 cup ricotta cheese  
    1½ cups shredded mozzarella  
    ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Tools

- Dutch oven
- Large pot (for boiling pasta)
- Wooden spoon
- Knife and cutting board
- Colander
- Oven mitts

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook pasta until just shy of al dente. Drain.
3. Heat olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium heat.
4. Add sausage or beef and cook until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Add onion and cook until softened.
6. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
7. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute.
8. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, and basil. Simmer 10 minutes.
9. Stir in cooked pasta and ricotta.
10. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan.
11. Transfer Dutch oven to the oven, uncovered.
12. Bake 25–30 minutes until bubbling and golden on top.
13. Rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Undercook pasta slightly to prevent mushiness.
- Sauce should be slightly loose before baking.
- Can be assembled ahead and baked later.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches moisture management and uncovered finishing.
You learn how pasta absorbs liquid during baking and how cheese
browns without drying the dish. Controlling sauce looseness
before baking ensures a cohesive, rich result rather than a
stiff or watery casserole.
