# Dutch Oven Beef Stew

## Ingredients

    2½ lb beef chuck, cut into 1½-inch cubes  
    2 tsp kosher salt  
    1 tsp black pepper  
    2 tbsp olive oil  
    1 large onion, diced  
    3 cloves garlic, minced  
    2 tbsp tomato paste  
    2 tbsp all-purpose flour  
    3 cups beef stock  
    1 tsp dried thyme  
    1 bay leaf  
    4 carrots, cut into chunks  
    3 celery stalks, sliced  
    1 lb potatoes, cut into chunks

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid
- Wooden spoon
- Tongs
- Knife and cutting board
- Measuring spoons

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Pat beef dry and season with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Brown beef in batches, ensuring deep color on all sides. Remove to a plate.
5. Lower heat to medium. Add onion and cook until softened.
6. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
7. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1–2 minutes until darkened.
8. Sprinkle flour over the vegetables and stir to coat; cook 1 minute.
9. Slowly add beef stock, scraping up browned bits.
10. Return beef to the pot. Add thyme and bay leaf.
11. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and transfer to oven.
12. Cook for 1½ hours.
13. Add carrots, celery, and potatoes. Cover and return to oven.
14. Cook another 45–60 minutes until beef and vegetables are tender.
15. Remove bay leaf, adjust seasoning, and rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Browning in batches prevents steaming and builds flavor.
- Flour provides body; omit for a clearer, brothier stew.
- Stew improves after a night in the fridge.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches ingredient sequencing and texture control. By browning
meat separately, thickening early, and adding vegetables later, you learn
how to manage multiple doneness timelines in one pot. This discipline
prevents overcooking and yields a stew with depth, clarity, and
structure—essential for complex one-pot meals.
