# Dutch Oven Foundational Techniques (Progression Order)

## Steam-Assisted Baking (Bread)

- Preheat pot fully
- Dough supplies its own steam
- Remove lid late for crust
- Transferable skill: any hearth-style bread

## Sear → Deglaze → Braise (Chicken, Roasts)

- Dry protein = better browning
- Don’t move meat until it releases
- Deglaze while pan is still hot
- Liquid halfway up protein, not submerged

## Flavor Layering (Chili, Stews)

- Bloom spices in fat
- Tomato paste needs cooking
- Salt in stages
- Simmer ≠ boil

## Starch Management (Risotto)

- Hot stock only
- Stir gently, not constantly
- Final texture should flow
- Creaminess comes from starch, not cream

## Ingredient Sequencing (Stews)

- Brown meat first, remove
- Aromatics second
- Tough vegetables late
- Herbs early, fresh finishes late

## Reduction Discipline (Short Ribs, Wine Braises)

- Reduce wine before stock
- Taste after reduction
- Fat floats—skim if needed
- Acid sharpens richness

## Moisture Control (Baked Pasta)

- Sauce slightly loose pre-bake
- Undercook pasta
- Bake uncovered for browning
- Rest before serving

## Full Braise Orchestration (Osso Buco)

- Aggressive sear
- Low, steady oven heat
- Collagen → gelatin transformation
- Finish with contrast (acid, herbs)
