# Dutch Oven Chili

## Ingredients

    2 tbsp olive oil  
    1 lb ground beef (or beef/pork mix)  
    1 tsp kosher salt  
    1/2 tsp black pepper  
    1 medium onion, diced  
    3 cloves garlic, minced  
    2 tbsp chili powder  
    1 tsp ground cumin  
    1 tsp smoked paprika  
    1/2 tsp dried oregano  
    1/4 tsp cayenne (optional)  
    1 tbsp tomato paste  
    1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes  
    1 (15 oz) can kidney or pinto beans, drained and rinsed  
    1 cup beef stock or water

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid
- Wooden spoon
- Knife and cutting board
- Measuring spoons

## Steps

1. Heat olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
2. Add ground meat, season with salt and pepper, and cook until browned, breaking it up.
3. Add onion and cook until softened, 4–5 minutes.
4. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Push contents to the side, add chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, and cayenne
   directly to the hot surface.
6. Stir spices for 20–30 seconds until aromatic.
7. Add tomato paste and cook 1 minute to deepen flavor.
8. Stir in crushed tomatoes, beans, and stock.
9. Bring to a gentle simmer.
10. Reduce heat to low, cover partially, and simmer 45–90 minutes, stirring occasionally.
11. Adjust seasoning and thickness with salt or water as needed.

## Notes

- Longer simmering improves flavor; keep heat low to avoid scorching.
- Chili thickens as it cools—err slightly looser than you want.
- Optional additions: diced peppers, cocoa powder, or a splash of vinegar.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches flavor layering and spice blooming. Cooking spices
briefly in hot fat unlocks their aromatic compounds before liquid is
added. You also learn controlled simmering: low, steady heat that
develops depth without reducing everything into mush. These skills
apply broadly to soups, curries, and stews.
