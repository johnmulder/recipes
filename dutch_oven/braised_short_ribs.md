# Dutch Oven Braised Short Ribs

## Ingredients

    4 lb bone-in beef short ribs  
    2 tsp kosher salt  
    1 tsp black pepper  
    2 tbsp olive oil  
    1 large onion, chopped  
    2 carrots, chopped  
    2 celery stalks, chopped  
    4 cloves garlic, smashed  
    2 tbsp tomato paste  
    2 cups dry red wine  
    2 cups beef stock  
    2 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)  
    1 bay leaf

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid
- Tongs
- Wooden spoon
- Knife and cutting board
- Oven mitts

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Pat short ribs dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Heat olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
4. Sear short ribs on all sides until deeply browned, 3–4 minutes per side. Remove to a plate.
5. Reduce heat to medium. Add onion, carrots, and celery; cook until softened and lightly browned.
6. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
7. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1–2 minutes until darkened.
8. Pour in red wine and simmer 5–7 minutes to reduce by about half, scraping up browned bits.
9. Add beef stock, thyme, and bay leaf.
10. Return short ribs to the pot, bones up, meat mostly submerged.
11. Cover and transfer to oven.
12. Braise for 2½–3 hours, until meat is fork-tender.
13. Remove ribs and skim excess fat from sauce if desired.
14. Reduce sauce on stovetop if thicker consistency is preferred.
15. Return ribs to sauce and rest 10 minutes before serving.

## Notes

Bone-in ribs provide superior flavor and structure.
Reducing the wine sufficiently prevents harsh acidity.
Best served with mashed potatoes or polenta.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches deep browning and reduction discipline. You learn how
aggressive searing builds a foundation of flavor, while controlled wine
reduction concentrates richness without bitterness. Managing fat, acidity,
and gelatin-rich cuts together is a hallmark skill of confident Dutch
oven cooking.
