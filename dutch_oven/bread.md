# No-Knead Dutch Oven Bread

## Ingredients

    3 cups (360 g) all-purpose flour  
    1 1/2 tsp kosher salt  
    1/2 tsp instant yeast  
    1 1/2 cups warm water (about 105–110°F)

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid (5–7 qt, oven-safe)
- Large mixing bowl
- Wooden spoon or spatula
- Plastic wrap or clean towel
- Parchment paper
- Oven mitts

## Steps

1. In a large bowl, mix flour, salt, and yeast.
2. Add warm water and stir until a shaggy, sticky dough forms.
3. Cover the bowl and let rest at room temperature for 12–18 hours.
4. Turn the dough out onto parchment paper. With lightly floured hands, fold it over itself once or twice.
5. Shape gently into a rough ball and let rest, covered, for 30–60 minutes.
6. Preheat oven to 450°F with the empty Dutch oven (and lid) inside for at least 30 minutes.
7. Carefully remove the hot Dutch oven, lift the dough using the parchment, and lower it in.
8. Cover and bake for 30 minutes.
9. Remove the lid and bake another 10–15 minutes until deep golden brown.
10. Remove bread and cool on a rack for at least 30 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- The dough will be very sticky—this is normal.
- Longer fermentation (up to 18 hours) improves flavor and texture.
- For variation, add 1/2 cup seeds, olives, or grated cheese during mixing.
- Internal temperature when done is ~205-210 F.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches steam-assisted baking using a sealed vessel. The preheated
Dutch oven traps moisture released by the dough, allowing maximum oven spring
before the crust sets. Removing the lid partway through shifts the environment
from steaming to dry heat, creating a thick, crackly crust. Mastery here
translates directly to better hearth-style breads and understanding how
moisture and heat interact during baking.
