# Coq au Vin (Dutch Oven)

## Ingredients

    4 lb bone-in chicken pieces (thighs and drumsticks preferred)  
    1 ½ tsp kosher salt  
    ¾ tsp black pepper  
    4 oz bacon, diced  
    1 tbsp olive oil (if needed)  
    1 large onion, chopped  
    2 carrots, sliced  
    3 cloves garlic, smashed  
    2 tbsp tomato paste  
    2 cups dry red wine  
    1 cup chicken stock  
    2 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)  
    1 bay leaf  
    8 oz mushrooms, halved

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid
- Tongs
- Wooden spoon
- Knife and cutting board
- Oven mitts

## Steps

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Season chicken with salt and pepper.
3. Place bacon in the cold Dutch oven and cook over medium heat until
fat renders and bacon is crisp. Remove bacon to a plate.
4. Add chicken pieces skin-side down and sear until golden. Flip briefly, then remove.
5. Add onion and carrots; cook until softened.
6. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
7. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1–2 minutes until darkened.
8. Pour in red wine and simmer 5 minutes, scraping up browned bits.
9. Add chicken stock, thyme, bay leaf, and mushrooms.
10. Return chicken and bacon to the pot.
11. Cover and transfer to oven.
12. Braise for 45–60 minutes, until chicken is tender.
13. Remove lid for last 10 minutes if sauce needs reduction.
14. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Notes

- Use a drinkable wine; cooking concentrates flaws.
- Bacon replaces added salt—taste before adjusting.
- Traditionally served with potatoes or buttered noodles.

Technique Learned

This recipe teaches compound flavor building: layering fat (bacon),
protein fond (chicken), aromatics, and wine into a unified sauce.
You refine timing—what browns, what softens, and what merely
simmers—while maintaining balance between richness and acidity.
This is classical braising with tighter margins and higher payoff.
