# Dutch Oven Risotto

## Ingredients

    1 tbsp olive oil  
    2 tbsp unsalted butter  
    1 small onion or shallot, finely diced  
    2 cloves garlic, minced  
    1 ½ cups Arborio rice  
    ½ cup dry white wine  
    4–5 cups warm chicken or vegetable stock  
    3/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese  
    Salt, to taste  
    Freshly ground black pepper

## Tools

- Dutch oven
- Wooden spoon or silicone spatula
- Ladle
- Saucepan (for warming stock)
- Measuring cups

## Steps

1. Warm the stock in a saucepan and keep it hot but not boiling.
2. Heat olive oil and butter in the Dutch oven over medium heat.
3. Add onion and cook until soft and translucent, not browned.
4. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Stir in the rice and cook 1–2 minutes until the edges look translucent.
6. Pour in the wine and stir until mostly absorbed.
7. Add one ladle of warm stock and stir gently until absorbed.
8. Continue adding stock one ladle at a time, stirring frequently,
   allowing each addition to absorb before the next.
9. After 18–22 minutes, taste the rice—it should be tender with a slight bite.
10. Remove from heat and stir in Parmesan.
11. Season with salt and pepper.
12. Rest 2–3 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Keep the stock hot; cold liquid slows cooking and affects texture.
- The final consistency should be loose and flowing, not stiff.
- Variations: mushrooms (sautéed first), peas, asparagus, or shrimp.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches starch management and heat control. Gentle stirring
releases starch from the rice, creating creaminess without cream. You learn
to read doneness by texture rather than time and to maintain steady, moderate
heat—skills that carry over to sauces, grains, and delicate stovetop cooking.
