# Braised Chicken Thighs

## Ingredients

    6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs  
    1 tsp kosher salt  
    ½ tsp black pepper  
    1 tbsp olive oil  
    1 medium onion, sliced  
    3 cloves garlic, smashed  
    1 cup chicken stock  
    1/2 cup dry white wine (optional; replace with more stock if omitted)  
    2 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)  
    1 bay leaf

## Tools

- Dutch oven with lid
- Tongs
- Wooden spoon
- Knife and cutting board
- Oven mitts

## Steps

 1. Preheat oven to 325 F.
 2. Pat chicken thighs dry and season both sides with salt and pepper.
 3. Heat olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high heat.
 4. Place thighs skin-side down and sear until deeply golden, about 5–7 minutes.
    Flip and sear 2 minutes more.
 5. Remove chicken to a plate.
 6. Lower heat to medium. Add onion and cook until softened, scraping up browned bits.
 7. Add garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
 8. Pour in wine (if using) and simmer 1–2 minutes to reduce slightly.
 9. Add chicken stock, thyme, and bay leaf.
 10. Return chicken to the pot, skin-side up. Liquid should come about halfway up the thighs.
 11. Cover and transfer to oven.
 12. Braise for 35–45 minutes, until chicken is tender and cooked through.
 13. Optional: Remove lid and broil for 3–5 minutes to re-crisp skin.
 14. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- Bone-in, skin-on thighs are strongly recommended for moisture and flavor.
- If the sauce is thin, remove chicken and simmer on the stovetop to reduce.
- Serve with mashed potatoes, rice, or crusty bread.

## Technique Learned

This recipe teaches the core braising workflow:

- sear for flavor
- deglaze to capture fond
- then cook gently in a partially submerged environment

You learn how tough, flavorful cuts benefit from time and moisture—and
how keeping the skin above the liquid preserves texture while the meat
stays juicy. This technique underpins nearly all classic Dutch oven
eat dishes.
