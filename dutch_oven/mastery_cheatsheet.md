# Dutch Oven Mastery

## Core Strengths of the Dutch Oven

- Heavy walls = thermal stability
- Tight lid = moisture retention
- Stovetop → oven = seamless transitions
- One vessel = fond preservation

## Heat & Lid Rules (Memorize These)

- Lid ON → moist heat, collagen breakdown, gentle cooking
- Lid OFF → reduction, browning, thickening
- High heat → searing only
- Medium–low heat → everything else

## Timing & Temperature Defaults

- Searing: Medium-high stovetop
- Braising oven: 300–325°F
- Bread baking: 450°F preheated pot
- Low simmer: Bare bubbles only

## Common Failure Modes (and Fixes)

- Gray meat → Heat higher, dry meat
- Bitter sauce → Reduce wine longer, add stock later
- Mushy vegetables → Add later
- Thin sauce → Lid off, reduce
- Greasy mouthfeel → Skim fat, add acid

## Mental Model (Remember This)

Brown for flavor.
Cover for tenderness.
Uncover for concentration.
Time does the rest.
