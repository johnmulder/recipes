# Chile Paste

- Yield: Approximately 2 cups  
- Total Time: 30 minutes

## Ingredients

    6 dried ancho chiles, seeded and torn into pieces
    3 dried New Mexico red chiles, seeded and torn into pieces
    2 dried árbol chiles, stems and seeds removed
    2 cups low-sodium chicken stock
    Water, as needed

## Tools

- Instant Pot
- Blender
- Kitchen scissors
- Ice cube trays
- Freezer bags

## Steps

1. Prepare the chiles.
   - Remove stems and seeds.
   - Tear larger chiles into approximately 1-inch pieces.

2. Toast the chiles.
   - Set Instant Pot to Sauté (Normal).
   - Add chiles to the dry pot without oil.
   - Toast for 2–5 minutes, stirring frequently, until fragrant and slightly darkened.
   - Avoid burning.

3. Simmer.
   - Add chicken stock.
   - Simmer for 5–8 minutes until chiles soften.
   - Select Cancel.

4. Blend.
   - Allow mixture to cool slightly.
   - Transfer chiles and liquid to a blender.
   - Blend until completely smooth, approximately 2 minutes.
   - Add water if necessary to achieve a thick, pourable consistency.

5. Store.
   - Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
   - Alternatively, freeze in ice cube trays using 2 tablespoons per compartment.
   - Transfer frozen cubes to freezer bags and store for up to 1 year.

## Notes

- Substitution: Use 2 tablespoons chile paste for every 1 tablespoon chili powder, adjusting other seasonings as needed.
- Ancho chiles: Mild, earthy, slightly sweet.
- New Mexico red chiles: Mild to medium heat with bright chile flavor. California or guajillo chiles are suitable substitutes.
- Árbol chiles: Provide heat. Use 1 for mild, 2 for medium, or 3–4 for hotter paste.
- Instant Pot: Use Sauté mode only; pressure cooking is unnecessary.
